# Content origins

## Original project material

The React/TypeScript code, CSS, SVG geometry, rotor symbols, airflow paths, interface branding, animations, teaching text, tests and response model were created for this project. The SVG was designed from basic shapes to express researched connections; no manufacturer illustration was copied, traced or extracted. No company logos, photographs, audio, video, CAD or third-party 3D models are included. Fonts use the operating system stack; no font files or remote font service are used.

## Facts and names

Engineering facts were researched from the sources in SOURCES.md. Manufacturer, aircraft and engine names are factual identifiers. This project claims no ownership of trademarks or engineering facts and implies no endorsement. Source documents remain with their owners and are linked, not packaged. Public availability, attribution and an educational purpose are not treated as permission to reuse artwork.

## Reused software

React, React DOM and development dependencies are used under their package licences. THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md inventories every installed package with its version and declared licence and identifies preserved licence files. The production third-party notice includes React, React DOM and Scheduler copyright and licence text.

## Limitations / open questions

No unresolved rights question is known for an external media asset, because none was reused. This is a provenance record, not a legal opinion or a guarantee of legal compliance. The user has not selected a licence for the original project code; no public licence or ownership claim is implied by delivery. Publishing or redistributing a modified bundle requires preserving the applicable dependency notices and reviewing any new content.

Interface revision: original swept blade silhouettes replace abstract rotor marks. Twelve drawn silhouettes are a graphical sampling choice, not an engineering blade count. Optional audio is generated locally with Web Audio oscillators; no recordings are reused and no authentic engine acoustics are claimed.

Airflow revision: original converging intake paths and particle streaks. The section heat profile is an original dimensionless teaching model; no Celsius figures, manufacturer temperatures or material limits are claimed.

Instrument-bench revision: original metallic SVG shading, analog-style teaching gauges and live section heat callouts. The visual finish does not claim engineering geometry, true blade counts or authentic cockpit instruments.

Audio revision: distinct original synthesized profiles for the two engines, combining filtered generated noise with fan harmonics and spool tones. The Trent includes an IP tone; the GEnx has no IP tone. Profile pitches and timbres are creative teaching choices, not researched acoustic signatures. No recordings or other external audio assets are used.

README gallery: screenshots in docs/images are captures of this project's own locally rendered interface, produced with tests/readme/screenshots.spec.ts. They are not manufacturer photographs or generated mockups. Regenerate with npx playwright test --config playwright.readme.config.ts. These documentation assets are kept outside public/ and are not bundled into the website.
